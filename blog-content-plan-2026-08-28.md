# DecksRXKC: Five-Post SEO Content Plan

Prepared August 28, 2026. This plan uses the current site architecture, the supplied on-page SEO checklist, the existing two guides, and a live Kansas City SERP review. Keyword groupings are intent-led; search volume was not supplied and should be validated in Google Search Console and the preferred keyword platform before drafting.

## Strategy in one sentence

Publish five decision-stage guides that answer specific homeowner questions, then pass relevance and qualified traffic to the existing service, location, and project pages that own commercial searches.

## Search-intent ownership

| Intent | Primary owner | Role of the new post |
|---|---|---|
| Screened-in deck builder Kansas City | `/services/screened-in-decks` | Answer whether an existing deck can be enclosed |
| Composite deck builder / Trex deck builder Kansas City | `/services/composite-decks` | Explain cost drivers and compare Trex with TimberTech |

Do not create blog posts titled simply “Screened-In Decks in Kansas City,” “Composite Decks in Kansas City,” or “Covered Decks in Kansas City.” Those phrases already belong to service pages.

## Publishing standard from the supplied checklist

Every post should include:

- One primary intent, one H1, a short keyword-forward slug, canonical URL, unique title, and unique 145–160 character description.
- The primary answer in the first paragraph and the primary phrase within the first 100 words.
- A table of contents, logical H2/H3 structure, short paragraphs, and 1,500+ useful words where the topic earns that depth.
- Four to six visible FAQs with concise answers and matching FAQ JSON-LD; also include Article, BreadcrumbList, Organization, and author/person markup where the site supports them.
- Three to five contextual internal links, including the primary service page and relevant project/location proof.
- Two to three authoritative external citations placed next to the claims they support. Prefer municipal, code/standards, and manufacturer sources.
- Original project images where possible, descriptive filenames and alt text, explicit dimensions, WebP/JPEG compression, lazy loading below the fold, and a 1200×630 social image.
- A named reviewer with real deck-building credentials, a published date, and an updated date. Do not invent experience, licensing, project location, pricing, warranty, or permit claims.
- A primary CTA after the opening answer and another after the decision content; keep phone and quote actions available without interrupting the article.

## Measurement

Before publication, record query/page baselines in Search Console. Review at 14, 30, and 60 days for impressions, correct landing-page ownership, clicks, engaged sessions, quote-form starts, and calls. If a blog post begins outranking its service page for transactional variants, strengthen the post-to-service link and sharpen the distinction rather than creating another overlapping URL.
